# Meetings and booking

Meetings and booking in Margince covers how a contact books time with you, and what happens to the invitation after that. You set your hours, calendars and booking link in **Settings → Meetings**; see [Your own settings](your-own-settings.md#meetings) for that set-up.

There are three ways to set up a meeting with a contact. You can offer two or three times, send an invitation for a time you agreed, or share a personal link. **My booking link**, in the account menu, is a fourth way that needs no contact.

### How do I send a meeting invitation to a contact?
To send a calendar invitation in Margince, open the contact's **Meetings** tab, choose **Book a meeting**, and pick the time you agreed.
1. Under **How to arrange the meeting**, choose **Send an invite**.
2. Pick the **Agreed time** in the calendar.
3. Fill the **Meeting title**, and the **Location or meeting link** if there is no video call.
4. Choose **Send invite · {time}**.
The invitation goes out from your own calendar. You need a connected calendar that may send invitations.
Also called: send an invite, book a call, schedule a meeting, calendar invite.

### What do the invitation states mean?
A Margince meeting invitation shows one of three states on the meeting. **Creating your invitation…** means it has not reached your calendar yet. **Calendar invitation created** means your calendar provider accepted it and told the guests. **Invitation needs attention** means the calendar did not accept it, even after Margince tried again.

Calendar invitation created does not mean the guest said yes. The guest's reply comes through your connected calendar, as with any other invitation.
Also called: pending, confirmed, invite failed, delivery status.

### How do I fix an invitation that needs attention?
To fix a Margince invitation that shows **Invitation needs attention**, open the meeting from its line on the timeline and choose **Retry invitation**.
1. Read the reason under **The calendar did not accept the invitation**.
2. If your calendar is read-only or its connection ran out, reconnect it in **Settings → Connections**.
3. Choose **Retry invitation**.
Retry sends the same invitation again, so the guest does not get a second one. The time stays held for you while Margince is not sure the invitation went out.
Also called: resend invite, invitation failed, calendar refused.

### How do I move a meeting to another time?
To move a Margince meeting, open it from its line on the timeline and choose **Reschedule**. Pick the new time and choose **Confirm new time**.
Your current time stays held until the calendar accepts the change. The meeting moves in Margince only once the calendar provider takes the new time. A meeting you move keeps the video call it already has.
Also called: reschedule, change the time, postpone a meeting.

### How do I cancel a meeting?
To cancel a Margince meeting, open it from its line on the timeline and choose **Cancel meeting**. Confirm "Cancel this meeting and notify the attendee?".
The time becomes free once your calendar accepts the cancellation, or confirms the event is already gone. A meeting you move or cancel in your own calendar is copied back to Margince within about 15 minutes.
Also called: call off a meeting, delete a meeting.

### How do I offer a contact a few times to choose from?
To offer times in Margince, open the contact's **Meetings** tab, choose **Book a meeting**, then choose **Propose times**.
1. Pick two or three **Times to offer** in the calendar.
2. Choose **Review email**, read the email, and send it.
The contact gets a personal link with your times. It books one meeting, and runs out after 7 days or at the last time offered.
Also called: suggest times, propose a slot, send options.

The times you offer are not held in your calendar. When the guest picks one, Margince checks your calendar again before it books. The guest may also choose another free time from the same link. A guest who opens a used link again gets back the meeting they already booked, never a second one.

### How do I take back a personal link I sent?
To stop a personal link in Margince, open the contact's **Meetings** tab, open **More actions** on the link, and choose **Withdraw**. Confirm with **Withdraw invitation**.
The guest's link stops working at once. The tab lists only your own links that are not used, not run out and not withdrawn, newest first.
Also called: cancel a proposal, revoke a booking link, withdraw an invitation.

### How do I get a reminder email before a meeting?
To send your guest a reminder in Margince, open **Settings → Meetings** and turn on **Email reminder one hour before**. Then choose **Save settings**.
The reminder goes from your connected mailbox, an hour before each future meeting. It gives the time in your time zone and names that zone. The meeting page shows "Email reminder scheduled.", "Email reminder queued for delivery." or "The reminder could not be sent."
Also called: meeting reminder, remind the guest, notification before a call.

Queued means Margince asked your mailbox to send it, not that it reached the guest. Margince sends a reminder only within 15 minutes of its time, so one it missed shows as not sent. A cancelled meeting gets no reminder. The reminder follows the same email rules as any mail you send: a contact who may not get email gets none.

## What counts as busy

Your free times in Margince are your bookable hours minus everything your calendars show as busy. Busy means anything booked in the calendar for new meetings, and in your other calendars that block time. Internal meetings, private events, time you blocked to work alone, and all-day events marked busy all count. None of them becomes a record in Margince, and guests never see their titles, guests or text.

Open times start every 15 minutes in your time zone. Guests can choose the time zone they see times in.

If Margince cannot read a calendar it needs, it shows no times at all, rather than times that may be wrong. A search with no times says why: past your booking horizon, or inside your notice.

Margince checks your hours, notice and live calendar again each time a guest books or a meeting moves. It checks your calendar once more right before it writes the event. Another calendar app can still book the same time in the second between that check and the write.

## What your guest sees

The Margince booking page shows your name from **Settings → Account**, and your company name and logo from the company profile. When new meetings get a video call, it names the video app: Google Meet or Microsoft Teams. Guests never see which calendar provider you use, your calendar names, or other guests.

**Pause bookings** stops new bookings on your public page. Personal links you already sent keep working until they run out. **Replace booking link** stops the old public address, but guests keep the private links that let them change their own meetings.

Every calendar invitation carries a private link that lets the guest move or cancel the meeting. Anyone who can read the full invitation, such as a colleague who manages your calendar, can use that link too. Think about who can read your calendar.

What a guest writes in **What would you like to discuss?** on your booking page stays in Margince. It shows on the meeting in the contact's **History** tab, and not on the meeting page. The calendar invitation leaves it out. The invitation holds your **Meeting title** and **Location or meeting link**, any video link, and the guest's private link.

A guest gives consent to the meeting separately from any marketing consent, and does not have to give marketing consent to book.

## When the calendar adds no video call

**Add a video call link to new meetings** asks your calendar for the link when the meeting is created. Google Calendar adds Google Meet. Outlook adds its own default, Microsoft Teams for a work or school Microsoft 365 account.

The link can take a few seconds to appear. Until then the meeting says "The link appears once the calendar accepts the invitation." If the calendar makes no link, the invitation still goes out. The meeting says "The calendar did not add a video link. Add one in your calendar."
