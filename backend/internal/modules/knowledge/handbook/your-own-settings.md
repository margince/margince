# Your own settings

Your own settings in Margince are the six pages under **You** in Settings:
**Account**, **Meetings**, **Writing voice**, **Agents**, **Connections** and
**Capture activity**. Everything here changes your own seat and nobody else's.
Two pages, Connections and Capture activity, also carry one card each that holds
for the whole company.

For everything else, see [Settings](settings.md).

### How do I open my own settings?
To open your own settings in Margince, open the account menu at the top right and choose **Settings**, then pick a page under **You**.
The six pages are **Account** (name, password, signature, language, appearance), **Meetings** (bookable hours, calendars and booking choices), **Writing voice**, **Agents**, **Connections** and **Capture activity**.
You can also press ⌘K (Ctrl+K) and type the page name, or a word such as "password", "language", "theme" or "mailbox".
Also called: my profile, my account, preferences, user settings.

### How do I change my notifications?
Margince has no notification bell and no notification settings. The two summaries that come each day and each week are the morning brief and the weekly review. You choose whether they also come by email. Open **Settings → Account** and set **Morning brief** and **Weekly review** to **By email** or **Not by email**.

Both always appear on **Home**, whatever you pick, and what needs you waits on your Worklist there.
Also called: email notifications, turn off emails, alerts, daily digest.

## Account

The Account page in Settings holds one card, **Your account**. It holds who you
are, how you sign in and how you sign off. It also holds which language the
product uses with you (English, Deutsch or Tiếng Việt), and how it looks. It
also has **Sign out**.
**Appearance** is here as well as in the account menu, where it is called
**Theme**. Both change the same setting.

### How do I change my password?
To change your password in Margince, open Settings → **Account** and choose **Change password** in the **Password** row.
1. Enter your **Current password**.
2. Enter a **New password** of at least 12 letters or signs, and type it again in **Confirm new password**.
3. Choose **Save new password**.

Margince says **Password changed** and signs you out on all your other computers and phones.
Forgot it? Use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page where it is shown, or ask an administrator for a new link to set a password.
Also called: reset my password, update login.

### How do I change my display name?
To change your display name in Margince, open Settings → **Account**, edit the **Display name** field and choose **Save**.
Your display name is what colleagues see on records you edit, in pickers and in the audit log.
It cannot be empty, and it can be up to 255 letters or signs long. Your email address shows under it, and you do not change it here.
Also called: my name, profile name, rename myself.

### How do I add an email signature?
To add an email signature in Margince, open Settings → **Account** and choose **Edit signature** in the **Email signature** row.
1. Type your sign-off in **Sign-off**, in plain text.
2. Choose **Save**.

Margince adds the signature under every email you send yourself, above the unsubscribe footer. Leave it empty and your emails end with a short sign-off and your name instead, in the message's language. When the message is too short to tell, that is the installation's language, then English. The row then says **No sign-off set**.

An email an agent sends carries no sign-off. AI drafts never add a sign-off, so this is the one that goes out.
Also called: email footer, sign-off, signature block.

### How do I change the language?
To change the language Margince uses with you, open Settings → **Account**. Pick one in the **Language** row: **English**, **Deutsch** or **Tiếng Việt**.
The screen changes at once. The choice is saved to your account, so your next sign-in uses it too, and it changes nothing for anyone else.
The sign-in page has its own language switch at the foot of the page.
Also called: change to German, switch to Vietnamese, locale, translation.

### How do I change the theme?
To change the theme in Margince, open Settings → **Account** and pick a choice in the **Appearance** row: **Light**, **Dark** or **System**.
You can do the same from the account menu at the top right, under **Theme**. Both change the same setting.
**System** follows the light or dark setting of your computer or phone, and keeps following it.
Also called: dark mode, light mode, appearance, colour scheme.

## Meetings

### How do I set my bookable hours?
To set when customers can book time with you in Margince, open Settings → **Meetings** and fill in the **Bookable hours** card.
1. Set **Day starts** and **Day ends**.
2. Tick your **Working days**.
3. Choose a place in the **Timezone** list, which shows the time zone your free times use now.
4. While the list is open, type the place name to go to it.
5. Choose **Save working hours**.

Until you save, the card says **Not set yet**, and customers are offered 09:00 to 17:00, Monday to Friday, in the installation's time zone.
Also called: working hours, availability, office hours.

The Bookable hours card is personal: "Personal setting. Only you set your
hours." Nobody sets them for you, and you set them for nobody else. Narrowing
them says what it costs: "Customers have fewer times to choose from."

### Calendar and booking preferences

Open **Settings → Meetings** to set working hours and time zone, and the
calendar that gets invitations. You also pick other calendars whose busy events
block times. With
one connected calendar provider, Margince picks it by itself. With both Google
Calendar and Microsoft Outlook connected, choose the provider here. You must
reconnect a read-only connection before it can send invitations.

On this page you set how long a meeting lasts, and the least notice a guest must
give. You also set the free time around a meeting, and how far out guests can
book. Guests cannot book dates past that; move it out here to offer later dates.
Margince keeps clear of all-day events marked busy. In the booking form, **Find
next available times** searches the rest of that time.

**Add a video call link to new meetings** is on by default. Your calendar makes
the link: Google Meet for Google Calendar, and the Outlook default, often
Microsoft Teams, for Outlook. You can turn it off for one meeting while you book
it. If the calendar adds no link, the invitation still goes out.

**My booking link** is still the place to copy a public link you can use again
and again, for your email signature. There you can also look at it first, pause
it or replace it. Opening meeting settings from a booking draft opens another tab;
coming back loads the saved settings again.

### How do I share a booking link?
Open **Settings → Meetings**. Connect or reconnect Google Calendar or Outlook Calendar with permission to create invitations. Your name comes from **Settings → Account**, and your company's look comes from **Settings → Company**. Set the meeting details and the limits on your free times, then save. Open the account menu → **My booking link** and choose **Enable bookings**.

A calendar marked **Read-only calendar** is connected to read events, but cannot
send invitations. In Settings → Connections, choose **Reconnect** and allow it
to send invitations. Choose the calendar for events in Settings → Meetings.
Choose **Copy link** to share it in any place, your email signature too. Guests
choose a time and enter their own details; you do not need to pick a contact
first.

**Preview public page** shows what guests will see. **Pause bookings** stops new
bookings without changing the link. **Replace booking link** stops the old link
working, so update any signatures or pages where you shared it.

### How do I arrange a meeting with a contact?
Open the **Meetings** tab of the contact and choose **Book a meeting**. Suggest two or three times and check the email before you send it. Or share a personal booking link, or send an invitation for a time you already agreed.
Personal links run out, and can book one meeting.
Suggested times and personal links that still wait on a reply are listed on the same tab. **Withdraw** stops a link working before the guest uses it.

Your free times take private and internal busy time into account, without
copying those details into Margince. Margince checks bookable hours, notice and
free time around meetings when a guest books. **Creating your invitation…**
means it has not gone out yet. **Calendar invitation created** means the
calendar provider accepted it. The guest saying yes is a separate event. Open
the meeting from its line on the timeline to move it, cancel it, or try again
when sending it needs your help.

## Writing voice

The Writing voice page in Settings holds your **Voice DNA**: your personal
writing voice. It shapes drafts written for you, only you can see it, and it
learns only from samples you add.

### How do I set up my writing voice?
To set up your writing voice in Margince, open Settings → **Writing voice**, add writing samples, then choose **Build Voice DNA**.
1. Under **Writing samples**, choose **Add writing samples** and drop or pick files, several at once.
2. The files can be `.txt`, `.md`, `.pdf`, `.docx`, `.vtt`, `.srt` or `.json`.
3. Add at least 800 words of your own writing. The bar shows how far you are.
4. Choose **Build Voice DNA**. It takes about 60 seconds, and goes on if you leave the page.

After the first build, the button reads **Rebuild Voice DNA**. Notes on how you want to sound go in **Preferences**, then **Save preferences**.
Also called: tone, writing style, Voice DNA, sound like me.

The best samples are emails you sent, then proposals and posts, then the written
record of calls or meetings. Leave out text others wrote, and AI drafts: Margince
learns a different voice from them. A read-only seat sees the page and cannot change it.

Margince reads a PDF or Word document to its text in the browser before it
sends anything. It skips a file with no text in it. It names a file it cannot
open (one with a password, or one that is broken), so you can copy its text in
instead.

When at least half of a file comes from named speakers, Margince reads it as a
conversation. The page asks which speaker is you, and keeps only your parts.
Under that share, the file counts as plain text and is taken whole. So an email
with a line such as "Frage: …" is not asked about.

## Agents

The Agents page in Settings is where you make and take back **passports**: the
keys that let an AI agent work as you. Every member can open this page.

### How do I create an agent passport?
To create an agent passport in Margince, open Settings → **Agents** and choose **New passport** in the **Agent passports** card.
1. Enter an **Agent name**.
2. Under **Agent permissions**, tick at least one: **Read records**, **Draft messages**, **Change records**, **Send messages**, **Buy contact data**.
3. Choose **Mint passport**.

An agent can never do more than you can: every request checks your own permissions again.
Also called: API key, access token, connect an AI agent.

You also see the list of actions agents may use, and the connected agents, on the
Agents page. Taking an agent off ends the whole connection, not one key. The
agent loses access on its next call and cannot get it back. Connecting it again
means approving access again.

See [Agents, passports and what they may
do](agents-and-passports.md#passports-how-an-agent-is-connected).

## Connections

The Connections page in Settings holds the mailbox and calendar you connected,
and your LinkedIn import. Connections is what *you* connected; **Integrations**
is what the *installation* is connected to.

### How do I connect my mailbox?
To connect your mailbox or calendar to Margince, open Settings → **Connections** and choose **Add connector** in **Connected mailboxes and calendars**.
1. Pick a provider: **Gmail**, **Google Calendar**, **Outlook**, **Outlook Calendar** or **IMAP mailbox**. You connect a calendar apart from its mail.
2. Choose **Connect** and approve access at the provider.

When it works, Margince says "Connected. Your mailbox is capturing."
Also called: link my email, sync Gmail, sync Outlook, connect my calendar.

All the detail (IMAP settings, importing past mail, reconnecting, and what
capture files where) is in [Connecting your mailbox and calendar](connecting-mail-and-calendars.md#what-you-can-connect).

## Capture activity

The Capture activity page in Settings holds two things: the senders you keep
out, and what the last 24 hours of your mail turned into.

**Capture exclusions** are addresses and domains whose messages never come into
the CRM. Your rules apply only to mailboxes you connected. The company's rules
apply to everyone, and only an administrator may add or remove one of those. A
rule applies from the next message; what is already captured stays.

**Outcomes** are counts for those 24 hours: **Captured**, **Dropped as
internal**, **No contact created**, **Awaiting sender check** and **Derivation
failed**. Click one to narrow the list under it.

**Messages**, behind a button, is the log for each message: which step a message
stopped at, and why. Open it when a message you expected did not show up. By
default each row names the sender and a short subject. If your installation
turned this off, the page says so above the rows.

### Why did an email not show up in Margince?
To find out why an email did not show up in Margince, open Settings → **Capture activity** and look in **Messages** for the last 24 hours.
1. Click an **Outcomes** count, such as **Dropped as internal** or **No contact created**, to narrow the list.
2. Open a row's steps to see where that message stopped, and why.

Mail between colleagues is never stored, and a sender under **Capture exclusions** is always kept out.
Also called: missing email, email not captured, email not synced.
