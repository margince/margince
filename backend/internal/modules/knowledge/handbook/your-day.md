# Your day

**Home** is where a day starts in Margince. It has two views, **Morning** and
**Weekly**, and two scopes, **Mine** and **Team**. The team scope appears only
if you can see records past your own. The week, its review and its plan are in
[Your week](your-week.md).

The Worklist is the ranked to-do list in Margince: one list of everything
waiting on you, in the order Margince ranks them. That is buyers to answer,
tasks due, promises made, deals at risk and items to check. It lives on **Home**.
Press **Show Worklist** to open it as a panel over the page; **Hide Worklist** closes it.
It has no sidebar row of its own.

## Daily tasks

### How do I open my worklist?
To open your Worklist in Margince, go to **Home** in the sidebar and press **Show Worklist**. The
Worklist opens as a panel headed **Worklist**, with the day's count on the button.
1. Click **Home** in the sidebar.
2. Press **Show Worklist**. Press **Hide Worklist** to close it.
3. A team lead can switch **Whose work** between **Mine**, **Unassigned**, **Team** and **All**.

There is no separate Worklist page, Today page or Inbox page. Also called: to-do list, my tasks,
work queue, inbox, today list.

### How do I snooze something on my worklist?
To snooze a Worklist item in Margince, press **Snooze** on its row. It comes back tomorrow ("Back on
your list tomorrow.").
1. Open **Home** → **Show Worklist**.
2. On the row, press **Snooze** for one day. Or open the arrow beside it (**Snooze duration**) for
   more choices.
3. Choose **Snooze for 3 days**, **Snooze for 7 days** or **Until they reply**.
4. The note that follows offers **Undo**.

A snooze changes your list only. A reply that came in before you snoozed does not end it. Also
called: remind me later, postpone, hide for now.

### How do I pin an item to the top of my worklist?
To pin a Worklist item in Margince, press **Pin** on its row. It moves to the top of your Worklist.
1. Open **Home** → **Show Worklist**.
2. Press **Pin** on the row. Press **Unpin** to put it back in its ranked place.

A pin changes the order of your own list only, and changes nothing for colleagues; how urgent it is
stays the same. You hold at most 50 pins: a newer one takes the place of your oldest. If it fails:
"Item was not pinned. Retry." Also called: star, flag, keep on top.

### How do I remove something from my worklist?
To take an item off your Worklist in Margince, open **Remove from list** on its row and choose **Not
mine** or **Not a customer**.
1. Open **Home** → **Show Worklist** and find the row.
2. Open **Remove from list**.
3. Choose **Not mine** ("Removed from your list. The owner still sees it.") or **Not a customer**
   ("Removed from every list.").
4. Press **Undo** in the note if you picked wrong.

**Not a customer** removes the whole thread for everyone, and does not run out. Also called:
dismiss, delete from worklist, hide, not relevant.

Archiving a contact also takes the messages they wait on a reply to off your Worklist. Restoring the
contact brings them back. Only the contacts you can see count. A message stays if it is filed under another
contact you can see who is not archived. It also stays if you can see none of its contacts.

### How do I undo a snooze or removal on my worklist?
To undo a Worklist snooze or removal in Margince, press **Undo** in the note that appears right
after you picked it.

The undo reaches as far as the choice did. Undoing your own snooze never brings back a thread a
colleague marked **Not a customer** for the whole company. If the undo fails, the item stays off
your list ("Undo failed. The message is still off your list."). Snoozed items also come back on
their own when the snooze ends. Also called: restore, bring back, unsnooze.

### How do I see all my tasks?
To see all your open tasks in Margince, open **Home**, press **Show Worklist**, and choose the
**Tasks** button under **Work type**.
Tasks are in groups by when they are due. Late ones and today's come first, then **Due tomorrow**,
**Due this week** and **Later**. There is no separate "Due today" heading.

A task more than 30 days late leaves the Worklist and its counts. A pin keeps it there while the pin
is one of your 50 newest. The task stays open, keeps its due date, and still shows on its record.
A company also has a **Tasks** tab with its own tasks; a contact has no Tasks tab.
Also called: my to-dos, tasks due today, open tasks, task list.

### How do I complete a task?
To complete a task in Margince, press **Done** on its Worklist row, or on the task in the record's
list of next steps.
1. Open **Home** → **Show Worklist**, or open the contact, company or deal the task is on.
2. Press **Done**. The task is marked "Task completed".
3. Not ready yet? Press **Snooze 1 day** to move its due date a day later. Or open the task and pick
   a day under **Move to**.

**Snooze 1 day** adds a day to the task's own due date, so a task with no date does not show it. If
it fails: "Task was not completed. Retry." Also called: tick off, mark done, close a task.

### How do I plan my week?
To plan your week in Margince, open **Home**, switch the view to **Weekly** (scope **Mine**) and add
items under **This week’s commitments**.
1. Click **Home**, then **Weekly**.
2. Under **This week’s commitments**, press **Add commitment** and fill in **Commitment**.
3. If you like, add a **Due date** and a linked deal, lead, contact, company or project, then press
   **Add**.
4. Tick commitments to mark them, then press **Save {count} changes**.
5. Fill in **Constraints this week**: **Risks** and **Available capacity**.

Also called: weekly plan, weekly goals, set commitments. More in [Your week](your-week.md).

### How do I see my weekly review?
To read your weekly review in Margince, open **Home** and switch the view to **Weekly**. The review
of your last closed week sits above your plan.

Margince writes the review on the Monday after the week, and then it is **Frozen**; you cannot make
it again. Before your first full week it says "No weekly review yet. The first one is created on the
Monday after your first full week." A team lead switches the scope to **Team** for the team's week.
Also called: weekly report, week in review, weekly summary.

## Morning

### What is on Morning?
**Morning**, on **Home**, shows what to do first today, in four parts. The time under it says when
the brief was made, and the agenda time says when the list was last read.
- **Focus**: at most 6 cards, in the Worklist's own order. Proposals to answer, commitments due
  today and meetings to prepare in the next 24 hours sit here. Tasks due later never fill a card. A
  quiet day says "No immediate priorities. The full Worklist is available."
- **Updates**: notices that only tell you something. A stage change notice names the deal, the stage
  before and after, and who moved it. You get none for a move you made yourself.
- **Context**: the column with **Upcoming meetings**. A task opens in the task dialog and a contact
  in a side panel, so the page keeps its place.
- **Show Worklist**: the whole list, with its own filters and pages, and work that is not due yet.
Also called: morning brief, daily brief, today, priorities, top cards.

### What comes first on Morning?
A customer who waits for your reply, and a reply deadline that comes close, come first on Morning
and in the Worklist.
- A message that waited more than 14 days is old work to recover, not today's. On an open deal, it
  keeps the rank of a deal at real risk.
- A privacy duty joins the day 7 days before its deadline; the legal deadline does not move.
- A privacy duty recorded more than a day after its deadline came from imported history. It reads
  "recorded after its deadline, from imported history", ranks as normal work and never takes a
  **Focus** card.
- A task due today or late is urgent, unless it is prospecting work.
- A deal at risk is urgent when its value is high next to the risk on all deals. It is also urgent
  when its expected close is past or within 14 days.
- Work for current customers comes before prospecting. A lead with no reply target shows "no
  response target set", with no made-up deadline.
- A close date Margince set for now asks you to confirm the forecast; it is not a customer promise.
  See [Close dates Margince sets](the-pipeline.md#close-dates-margince-sets).

### How complete is Morning?
Morning names each source it could not read, with **Refresh brief**, and never shows it as a zero.
- **Work summary** adds up deal value without the deals that have no price, and says how many those are.
- That value may not be complete, and it is not a forecast.
- Meetings count calendar entries still to come. Margince does not say you are prepared unless the
  source says so.

### Changes made for you
**Changes made for you**, on **Morning**, lists the changes Margince made to your deals that you can
still accept or undo. Each names the deal, what changed, why and when. A close date fix names the
old and new date; a change in confidence alone is not called a date change. **Accept** records that
you reviewed the change and keeps what it set. It does not run the change again, or turn a guessed
date into a customer promise. **Undo** puts the deal back, and the row then reads **Undone**.

A deal that changed again since reads "This deal changed again. Open the deal to review its current
state." Neither an undone change nor a replaced one can be accepted. To make Margince ask you first,
see [Approvals](approvals.md). Also called: automatic changes, review changes, accept a change.

### What happens to a promise from a call or an email?
A promise Margince reads in a meeting transcript or an email becomes something to watch or a task,
by who made it.
- A promise the customer made goes on their contact to watch, and never becomes a task. The deal
  lists open ones under **Customer commitments**, with **Open message**. **Dismiss** removes a wrong
  reading, if you may change the contact.
- A promise a named colleague made becomes their task. When Margince reads it clearly, it writes the
  task at once. If not, the colleague gets an **Add commitment task** proposal.
- A promise nobody can be named for goes as a proposal to the user whose call or mail it was.
- A promise in private mail stays with the owner of that mail. Its task is visible to its holder alone.
- In an email, only the words the sender wrote count, not text quoted from earlier mail.
- The task has a due date only when the conversation named a day. If not, it waits in today's list
  with **No due date**.
- Marking the task **Done** marks the promise kept, and the other way round. Opening the task again
  leaves the promise kept.
- A task you archived, a proposal you refused and a customer promise you dismissed do not come back
  when Margince reads the conversation again.
Also called: action items, follow-ups from a meeting, commitments in an email.

### What happens to a request in an email?
When a customer email asks you for something and nobody answered, Margince files a task for you with
**No due date**.
- It needs a clear request. A note that only tells you something, or one Margince cannot judge,
  stays in review and does not claim to be urgent.
- The task takes the place of the email in your Worklist, and its source opens the email. If you can
  no longer open the email, you cannot read the task either.
- Completing the task closes the request for every reader. Archiving it before it is done sends the
  request back to review, with no new task.
- A reply closes only its own conversation, and does not prove the promised thing was sent. The task
  stays until someone handles it.
- Private mail is left out. When it is not clear whose task it is, Margince leaves the email for review.
Also called: customer request, email nobody answered, email to-do.

## The Worklist

A lead record does not come into the Worklist because someone created it, imported it or set its
owner. A planned task linked to a lead, or a request that came in, can come in through the list of
tasks or of waiting messages. Choose prospects in
**Leads**, then plan when to reach out.

The Worklist is one ranked list. Margince sets the order, and the screen never changes it.

### The three bands

Worklist items are in bands, shown in this order:

| Band | When it is clear it says |
|---|---|
| **Now** | "No urgent items. Remaining work is below." |
| **Keep momentum** | "No agreed work is drifting." |
| **Prospecting** | "No prospecting work waiting." |

Work to check is one more kind. It does not show a band in the list; it has its own panel under it,
headed **To review**.

A band you have cleared still shows its heading and says so, but only when the whole day is loaded.

Inside a band, work due tomorrow, this week or later has its own heading. Late work and today's work
sit under the band heading, because that is what the band already means.

A row that does not fit moves down, never up. When one band fills, the rows that do not fit drop to
Keep momentum. They never go before work that was already urgent.

### Why a row is where it is
Each Worklist row explains itself three ways, in sentences, not under headings:

- **The facts that put it there**: a buyer wrote last, a promise is due, a meeting is close, nobody
  has replied in so many days.
- **Why it ranks above the row under it**, written out: "Ranked above the next item because you
  pinned it."
- **What happens if you leave it**: "If ignored, the buyer keeps waiting."

The rank comes from a fixed ladder of checks, not from one number. A pin comes first, then the band,
then whether the row did not fit its band. Next comes what
**kind** of work it is. It may be waiting on someone, a promise, a risk to real money, something
agreed, something that holds up a colleague, or daily work. Then comes its due date, then the money
it may bring, and so on down.

Each step decides before the next one is looked at. Nothing adds up into a single number you cannot
take apart.

### Pinning

A pin moves a Worklist row to the top of your Worklist and keeps it there. It is yours alone:
pinning changes the order of what you can already read, and changes nothing for anyone else. How
urgent it is stays the same. The audit log still records the pin.

**A pin never runs out.** It lasts longer than the row it names, and nothing takes one off for you.
The one exception: only your 50 newest pins count, so pinning one more makes your oldest stop
counting.

### Taking something off the list

Taking an item off the Worklist uses three choices, and they reach different numbers of users:

| Choice | What it does | Who it touches |
|---|---|---|
| **Snooze** | Comes back tomorrow, in 3 days, in 7, or when they reply | **Your list only** |
| **Not mine** | "Removed from your list. The owner still sees it." | **Your list only** |
| **Not a customer** | "Removed from every list." | **Everybody** |

"Not a customer" is judged on the **thread**, not the message, so the next reply in it comes in
already judged.

Each says what it did ("Back on your list tomorrow.", "Back on your list when they reply.") and each
offers **Undo**. The undo reaches as far as the choice did. Clearing your own snooze never lets back
in a thread a colleague ruled out for the whole company.

A reply that came in *before* you snoozed does not end the snooze.

### "List changed"

The Worklist holds still while you work through it. Something may come in, or someone may deal with
something while you work. Then a notice headed **List changed** says which: "{arrived} new since you
opened the list. Refresh to add them."

The page is still right, only not complete. The Refresh button appears only when something came in.

### How complete is this?
The Worklist footer says how complete the list is, in one of two ways:

- **"{shown} of {considered} shown"**: a plain share.
- **"{shown} shown · {sources} sources have more"**: a source stopped at its reading limit.

The second shows no share, because nobody knows the total.

If Margince could not read a source at all, a box headed
**Worklist incomplete** names it above the list. The empty list then reads
**"No items in the sources that loaded."** instead of "Nothing is waiting on you."

### Handled for you

**Handled for you**, on the Worklist, lists what Margince did for you in the last 24 hours: the
action, the record, when, and an **Undo**.

When there is nothing: "No actions taken on your behalf today."

**Undo** puts the record back the way it was before the change. It is offered on deals; other kinds
of entry show the row without one. An entry already undone says **"Already undone"** instead of
leaving the list, so you can see that it was.

## For a team lead

A team lead in Margince, or anyone who can see records past their own, sees more on Home and in the
Worklist.

On **Home → Team** you get the team board and the coaching suggestions. The rest lives in the
Worklist panel, which has its own **Whose work** switch (**Mine**,
**Unassigned**, **Team**, **All**). **Hidden from the Worklist** appears only at
**All**.

**Team exceptions** is team work that has gone past a line. A first reply is late, money is at risk,
nobody has taken it, or the same thing keeps failing. Each row names **what it was judged against**,
so you can see the bar instead of guessing it. You can take a row on from here.

**Team work needing attention** has a row per teammate: waiting on a reply, deals at risk, past due,
promises due. Unassigned work gets its own row.

**Coaching suggestions** has at most one suggestion per teammate: the most urgent kind, not the one
with the most work.

**Hidden from the Worklist** lists the reasons work is held back, each with what it costs you:
- **Too old for the Worklist**: no one decided this; the sender wrote months back and nobody answered
- **Not linked to a record**
- **From your company’s domains**
- **Marked not sales work**
- **Set aside by you**

When nothing is held back, it says: "Nothing is hidden. Every waiting customer reaches a Worklist."

You can also add a note to a teammate's Worklist with **Add note** ("A short note that appears in
their Worklist.").
