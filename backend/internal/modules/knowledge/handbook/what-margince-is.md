### What is Margince?
Margince is a CRM. It is one place for your contacts, the companies they work for, the leads you are still checking, and the deals you are working on. It also holds the projects you do after the sale.

It is different from other CRM products in three ways. Your AI agents work inside it, under your own permissions. It fills itself in from your connected mailbox and calendar. And it says clearly when it does not know something, instead of guessing.

You reach everything from the sidebar on the left, or from the command palette with ⌘K (Ctrl+K).
Also called: Margince CRM, Gradion CRM.

## 1. Your AI agents work inside it

In Margince, AI agents work inside the CRM. Most CRM products add AI as a panel that sums up what you typed in yourself.

In Margince, an AI agent connects to your company and gets a set of actions with
fixed rules. It can look things up, draft replies, read a document for you, add
facts to a company and move a deal on. Margince records everything it does, and
who it did it for.

The first rule fits in one sentence:

> An agent can do what the colleague behind it could do alone, and nothing
> more. Margince checks it against that colleague every time it acts.

An agent is nobody on its own. It acts for a colleague, with a passport
that colleague made and can take back. If you cannot see a record, neither can
your agent. If you may not do something, it cannot do it for you.

There is also a short list of things an agent can never do, whatever its
settings. It cannot, for one, approve a change that waits for a different
colleague. See [Agents, passports and what they may do](agents-and-passports.md).

[What the AI does, and what it does not →](what-the-ai-does.md)

## 2. It fills itself in

Margince fills itself in from your mail and calendar. You should not have to type into a CRM what you already wrote in an email.

Connect your mailbox and your calendar, and Margince files what comes in under
the right contacts, companies, deals and projects, by fixed rules. Where the
rules cannot decide, it asks you instead of picking.

[How conversations get in →](capture.md)

## 3. It tells you the truth about itself

Margince keeps "no" apart from "I do not know", and it says which one it means:

- When its documents do not cover a question, it answers **"Not covered by this
  set"** instead of making up something that sounds right.
- A Worklist with nothing in it says so: "Nothing is waiting on you."
- A field that was never changed says: "Set at creation and never changed. The
  audit log records no edits."
- A count taken from part of the data says it is part, so you do not read an
  empty field as a fact.
- Where nobody knows where a value came from, it says "source not recorded"
  instead of leaving it empty.

Every sentence the AI writes carries the records it was written from. Every
field the AI fills carries the text it was read from. Every number in a report
adds up from records you can open.

## What it holds

Margince holds these kinds of record:

- **Contacts**: the humans you sell to
- **Companies**: the companies they work for
- **Leads**: buyers you are still checking, kept apart from contacts until you
  check them
- **Deals**: business on its way to won or lost
- **Projects**: the work itself, which starts during the deal and goes on after
  it

There is also the timeline of emails, calls, meetings, notes and tasks that runs
through all of them. And there are the documents that build up over time.

## Getting around

The Margince sidebar is the list of screens down the left of the app. **Home**
sits on its own at the top. Under it the screens are in three groups:

- **Records**: **Contacts**, **Companies**, **Leads**, **Deals**
- **Work**: **Projects**, **Filters and views**
- **Intelligence**: **Analytics**

Settings is not in the sidebar: it is in the account menu at the top right. The
**Deals** row is the pipeline board; there is no separate Pipeline row. The
Worklist has no row of its own either; you open it from Home.

The sidebar has no counts or badges. The Worklist on Home shows those numbers.

### How do I get around the app?
To get around Margince, use the sidebar on the left. Or press ⌘K (Ctrl+K on Windows and Linux) to open the command palette, and type where you want to go.
1. Choose a screen in the sidebar: **Home**, then **Contacts**, **Companies**, **Leads**, **Deals**, **Projects**, **Filters and views** and **Analytics**.
2. Open **Settings** from the account menu at the top right.
3. To hide the sidebar, choose **Collapse sidebar**, or press ⌘B (Ctrl+B).

On a phone the sidebar turns into a bar at the foot of the screen. It holds **Home**, **Contacts**, **Deals**, the agent between them, and **More** for everything else.
Also called: navigation, menu, nav bar.

### Where is something in the sidebar?
Each Margince screen sits in one place in the sidebar, and you reach a few another way.
- Contacts, companies, leads and deals: the **Records** group.
- Projects, and saved filters and exports: the **Work** group, under **Projects** and **Filters and views**.
- Reports and the forecast: **Analytics**, under **Intelligence**.
- The Worklist, tasks and approvals: **Home**.
- The pipeline: the **Deals** row, which is the board.
- Settings: the account menu at the top right, not the sidebar.
- Scheduled messages, offers and search results: no sidebar row. Open them from the command palette (⌘K).

### How do I search for something?
To search Margince, click the search field **Search or ask Margince** in the top bar, or press ⌘K (Ctrl+K), and start typing.
1. Type a name, company, deal or any other word. Matches appear as you type, a few of each kind, under headings such as **Companies**, **Contacts** and **Emails**.
2. Choose a result to open it, or choose **See all results for “…”** to open the full **Search results** page.
3. On that page, **Show all** beside a kind, or **Show only**, lists every match of that kind.

Enter opens the row you moved to with the arrow keys. If you have not moved, Enter opens a screen or action that matches what you typed. If there is none, it opens a record whose whole name you typed, and if there is none, the **Search results** page. So Enter on part of a name shows the results instead of opening the first one.

If nothing matches, the palette says **No matches.** If record search fails, it says **Search failed**, and the screen commands still work.
Also called: find, look up, global search.

**Search results** are in groups by kind: Contacts, Companies, Deals, Leads,
Projects, Products, Offer templates, Emails, Activities and Tags. Each kind
shows its best few matches, so a company stays on the page, whatever the number of emails that
name it. A company's name also finds the contacts who work there now, each with
the line **Works at** and the company's name. They come after the contacts whose
own name matched. A result that came from a connected system is marked **From a
connected system**.

### What is the command palette?
The command palette is the Margince box that finds any screen, action, setting or record from one place. It opens over the page you are on, and its empty field reads **Search or ask Margince**.

Each row carries a badge. **Screen** marks a sidebar screen, and **Action** marks something it does (**New deal**, **Booking page**). **Record** marks a record that matches what you typed.
It also lists every settings page you may open, and **Scheduled messages**. It knows other words for a page: "price" finds **Products and offers**, and "password" finds **Account**.
Its first row is always **Ask your documents**.

### How do I open the command palette?
To open the Margince command palette, press ⌘K on a Mac or Ctrl+K on Windows and Linux. It works from any screen while no dialog is open.
You can also click the search field **Search or ask Margince** in the top bar.
Press ⌘K again, or Esc, to close it.
Also called: quick search, command menu, Cmd K, Ctrl K, keyboard shortcut.

### Where is the handbook, and how do I ask it a question?
The Margince handbook is inside the app. To ask it a question, open the command palette with ⌘K (Ctrl+K) and choose **Ask your documents**, the first row.
1. The **Ask your documents** dialog opens over the page, with whatever you typed so far.
2. **Document set** starts on your company's default set. That is the **Margince handbook** unless an administrator picked another. Change it to ask a different set.
3. Type in **Your question**, in your own words, and choose **Ask**.

The answer quotes the handbook and says where each part comes from. A question it does not cover gets **Not covered by this set** instead of a guess.
Also called: help, FAQ, knowledge base, ask Margince.

### How do I ask Margince a question about a company?
To ask about one company, open the company and use its **Ask about this company** panel. **Ask Margince** offers fixed questions (**What is open here?**, **Prepare for a meeting** and **What changed recently?**), answered from the records you can open. It takes no text of your own. For a question in your own words about how Margince works, use **Ask your documents** (⌘K).
Also called: company summary, meeting prep, what's going on with this account.

### Where is the Ask box?
The Margince Ask box is called **Ask your documents**, and it is the first row of the command palette. Press ⌘K (Ctrl+K), or click **Search or ask Margince** in the top bar.
It answers only from one document set: the **Margince handbook** that comes with every installation, or a set your company filed under Settings → **Knowledge**.
On a company page there is a second, separate panel, **Ask about this company**, with its own **Ask Margince** questions about that one company.
If the dialog says **No document sets**, there is nothing filed to search yet.

## Home and the Worklist

Home is where your day starts, and the way into the **Worklist**: the work that
waits on a human. That means decisions to answer, tasks to finish, copies of a
record to merge, and today's meetings. It also means deals going quiet, promises
you made, and what ran on its own during the night. Open it on Home with **Show
Worklist**. When there is nothing, the Worklist says "Nothing is waiting on
you." instead of showing an empty list.

## Words this handbook uses

**Company**: the word means two things, and the screen always makes clear which
one it means. Your *own* company is the tenant: all of your data in Margince,
and what a setting marked "Company" changes. A *company record* is a company you
do business with, listed under Companies.

**Installation**: the running copy of Margince your company uses. Whoever runs
it decides things like upload limits and whether sends need a second check. One
installation is for one company.

**Contact**: a human you do business with. The screen and the address bar both
say contact.

**Passport**: the key a colleague makes so an AI agent can act as them.

**Staged**: an action that has not happened yet, and waits for a human to
decide.

**Archived**: taken off the live lists, but still there. Margince archives
most things instead of deleting them. What really deletes data for good is a
short, clear list.

## Other words for the same thing

Margince uses one name for each thing. If you know it by another word, this
list gives the Margince name.

An opportunity is called a **deal** in Margince. Deals are in the **Deals** row of the sidebar, and the pipeline is the board on that screen.

An organisation, an organisation record, an account or a customer account is called a **company** in Margince. To add a new organisation record, open **Companies** in the sidebar, choose **New company**, enter the **Company name** and choose **Create**.

A customer or a client is a **company** you do business with, and the humans there are **contacts**. Margince has no separate customer or client record.

A prospect is called a **lead** in Margince. A lead is kept apart from contacts until you check it. Leads are in the **Leads** row of the sidebar.

A reseller, a referral partner or a channel partner is called a **partner** in Margince. That is a company you set up as a partner from its **More actions** → **Set up partner program**. A referral fee is its **commission**.

A proposal or a quote is called an **offer** in Margince. You create an offer from the **Offers** panel on a deal, and the price list behind it is called **Products and offers** in Settings.

A teammate or a colleague who signs in is called a **user** in Margince. You look after users in Settings → **Members**.

A knowledge base is called a **document set** in Margince. Document sets are filed in Settings → **Knowledge**.

The FAQ, the help or the handbook search is called **Ask your documents** in Margince. Open it from the command palette with ⌘K (Ctrl+K).

A to-do list or a task inbox is called the **Worklist** in Margince. It is on **Home**.

An API key or an access token for an AI agent is called a **passport** in Margince. You create one in Settings → **Agents**.

A writing style or tone profile is called **Voice DNA** in Margince. You set it up in Settings → **Writing voice**.

A theme, dark mode or light mode is called **Appearance** in Margince settings, and **Theme** in the account menu.

A workspace or tenant is your own **company** in Margince: everything a setting marked "Company" changes.

## Where it runs

Margince can run as a hosted service, or on your own servers. It can also run all
on one computer, the AI model too, for teams whose data cannot leave the
building. What this handbook says holds the same in all three.

Its license is BUSL-1.1, and you get the source.

### Is there a Margince mobile app?
There is no Margince app to get from an app store. Margince runs in the browser on a phone as well as on a computer. On a phone, a bar at the foot of the screen holds **Home**, **Contacts** and **Deals**, the agent between them, and **More** for everything else.
Dragging deal cards on the board does not work on touch screens; use the deal's **Stage** ladder instead.
Also called: iPhone app, Android app, phone app, use Margince on mobile.
