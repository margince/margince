# The compliance package, in English

The four documents in `../de/` are the ones to execute: they cite German
statutes, and a translation of a legal document is not the document. These
English versions are for reading and for internal circulation — so a founder,
an investor or a non-German-speaking colleague can see what the customer is
being asked to sign.

| German | English | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [mitarbeiterinformation.md](../de/mitarbeiterinformation.md) | [employee-information.md](employee-information.md) | What each employee is told before their mailbox is read (Art. 13) |
| [einwilligung-email-erfassung.md](../de/einwilligung-email-erfassung.md) | [consent-private-mail.md](consent-private-mail.md) | Consent, needed only where private use of work mail is permitted (§26(2) BDSG) |
| [betriebsvereinbarung-vorlage.md](../de/betriebsvereinbarung-vorlage.md) | [works-agreement.md](works-agreement.md) | The works council agreement (§87(1) Nr. 6 BetrVG) |
| [verarbeitungsverzeichnis-und-dsfa.md](../de/verarbeitungsverzeichnis-und-dsfa.md) | [processing-record-and-dpia.md](processing-record-and-dpia.md) | The Art. 30 record and Art. 35 impact assessment |

Margince checks none of them. See [the handbook](../../handbook/compliance.md)
for why that is a decision rather than an omission.
