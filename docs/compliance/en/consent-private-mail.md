# Consent to capturing private correspondence

> Reading copy. The [German version](../de/einwilligung-email-erfassung.md) is
> the one to execute. **Needed only where private use of the work mailbox is
> permitted or tolerated** — where it is effectively banned and the ban is
> enforced, §26(1) BDSG carries the processing and this document is unnecessary.

## Why consent is needed at all

An employer who permits or tolerates private use of a work mailbox is, when
reading that mailbox, also processing the correspondence of third parties who
have no employment relationship with them. The employment basis in §26(1) BDSG
does not stretch that far. So for that portion the processing rests on consent
under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR together with §26(2) BDSG.

## What the employee is agreeing to

That the company may capture the correspondence in their work mailbox through
Margince, including messages that are private in character — having been told:

1. **What is captured** — sender, recipients, subject, body and attachments,
   plus the contact records derived from them.
2. **Who can read it** — under the default `classified` setting, only them and
   the people on the message. A classifier then releases only ordinary business
   threads to the team. Correspondence judged personal or advisory stays held.
   An administrator cannot read held content.
3. **What they control** — exclude senders, destroy captured mail, see and
   correct every automatic decision on the Senders page, share or hold a single
   thread, disconnect the mailbox at any time.
4. **That it is voluntary.** They may refuse, and may withdraw at any time with
   future effect. **Neither refusal nor withdrawal carries any employment
   consequence.**
5. **What withdrawal does** — capture stops. Already-captured messages stay
   where a statutory retention duty applies; otherwise they can ask for
   destruction.

## Notes for whoever runs this

- **One consent per person per version.** If what the system does changes, ask
  again — a consent covers only what it informed about.
- **Document it, do not merely collect it.** Art. 7(1) requires proof: who
  signed which version, and when.
- **Margince verifies none of this.** It will connect a mailbox whether or not
  this page exists.
