# Information for employees: how your mail is captured

> Reading copy. The [German version](../de/mitarbeiterinformation.md) is the one
> to issue, and it must reach people **before** a mailbox is connected.

## What happens

When you connect your work mailbox to Margince, the system reads your business
correspondence and files it in the CRM so that work stays traceable when you are
away or leave.

**You connect it yourself.** Without your sign-in at the mail provider, the
system reaches nothing.

## Who can read it

This is the question that matters, and the answer depends on one setting:

- **`classified` (the default)** — every captured message is visible only to
  you and the people who were on the mail. A classifier then reads each thread
  and releases only the ordinary business conversations to your colleagues.
  Everything else stays private.
- **`held`** — nothing is ever released.
- **`shared`** — messages are readable by colleagues from arrival. Available
  only if management explicitly enables it.

**An administrator cannot read a held message.** The access check has no admin
exception.

If the classifier is unavailable, everything stays held. The system decides
against visibility when it cannot decide at all.

## What you control

- **Exclude a sender** so their mail is never captured.
- **Destroy what was already captured** — irreversibly, including attachments
  and everything derived from it. Mail a colleague also received stays theirs;
  your access to it ends.
- **See and correct every automatic decision** about your correspondents, on the
  Senders page. Your correction is final; the machine does not overwrite it.
- **Share or keep private a single thread**, whatever the classifier decided.
- **Disconnect the mailbox** at any time. What was captured stays until you
  destroy it.

## Retention

Commercial correspondence: as required by German statute. Everything else: the
period your employer sets. Mail judged personal is destroyed after a seven-day
undo window.

## Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, to the
contact your employer names. You may complain to a supervisory authority.

Consent, where you gave it, can be withdrawn at any time with future effect.
**Withdrawing it brings you no disadvantage.**
