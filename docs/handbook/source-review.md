# Review a contact source

Choose **Review source** on the contact or in the privacy queue. The panel shows the original record and the emails and meetings you may read, with their dates. Select a record and choose **View record** to read it, or describe other evidence. A record with no recorded source cannot prove where the details came from.

Answer how you received the details and check the preview before confirming. It shows whether a notice is due, its deadline and the time zone. A date based on when Margince recorded the contact is marked as the last date. The preview changes nothing. Confirming saves your answer and selected record; it checks your access again and refuses a preview whose evidence or answer changed.

**Met face to face** needs the day of the meeting. It records the meeting and supports personal follow-up mail inside the reply window, never marketing or a newsletter. No privacy notice is due. Other source answers grant no permission to send mail.

The contact's **What was said** card also shows recent notes you may read, with who wrote them when recorded. A note gives context and creates no task.
