# The trial

A Margince you installed yourself runs for 14 days with no licence. The days count from the day it
was set up. While they run, everything works and seats have no limit.

Members who may read the licence see how many days are left: on every page, under the agent at the
top of the rail, and on **Settings → Seats and license**. Admin and Ops hold that right by default.
The notice turns to a warning in the last week.

### How do I get a licence or more time?
Press **Talk to the Margince team** in the notice. Pick what would help (**More time**, **A license**
or **A short call**), add a line if you like, and press **Open in my mail app**. The email names your
company, the day the trial ends and how many seats are in use. Margince does not send it: your own
mail app does. **Copy text** copies it for any other mail program.
Also called: free trial, test licence, extend the trial, buy a licence, contact sales.

### What happens when the trial ends?
Margince stops working until it has a licence. There is no grace period. Every member sees **Your
Margince trial has ended** in place of the app, with the same button to write to the team, and
**Sign out**. Nothing is deleted, and your data is all there again once the licence is in place.
Your server admin puts the licence key into the installation's settings and starts it again. An
agent you connected stops too: every call it makes is refused.
Also called: trial ended, trial expired, Margince stopped working, locked.
