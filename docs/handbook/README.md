# The Margince handbook

How to use Margince day to day. It is written for whoever uses the product: a
rep, an account owner, a delivery lead, an administrator. All you need is a
sign-in and the app.

Start at [What Margince is](what-margince-is.md) and read on. Each page stands on
its own, so you can also come straight to the one you need.

To ask this handbook a question inside Margince, press ⌘K (Ctrl+K) to open the
command palette and choose **Ask your documents**. The **Margince handbook** is
the document set it answers from, unless an administrator chose another; then
pick **Margince handbook** in **Document set**.

## The pages

- [What Margince is](what-margince-is.md): what the product is for, and the
  three ways it is not like other CRM products. How to get around the sidebar,
  search and the command palette, and how to ask the handbook. It ends with a
  list of other words for the same thing.
- [Contacts, companies, leads, deals and projects](records.md): the five kinds
  of record, what each holds, and which links between them are required. How
  to create, edit, reassign, link and merge contacts and companies. Archiving,
  and why nothing is deleted. Notes and tasks, custom fields and tags. How to
  put a single change back from a record's history.
- [Leads, deals and projects](leads-deals-and-projects.md): how to create and
  edit a lead and change its owner. The lead ladder, what qualifying a lead
  does, reversing and disqualifying. How to create and edit a deal (value,
  currency, close date, company, project, owner). How to create, edit, reassign
  and close a project, and what a project page shows. Starting delivery, and
  choosing a name that gives a key you can read.
- [Lists, filters and views](lists-filters-and-views.md): searching, and
  filtering by owner, company, stage or tag. Sorting, table and board, showing
  archived records, and saving and opening views. Picking many records and
  reassigning them in bulk. More filters on Filters and views, and filters
  proposed from plain words. Live Lists and Shortlists: sharing, stewards, why
  a record is on a list, the 15-minute check, acting on members and automations.
  Exporting what a filter or a list finds.
- [The pipeline](the-pipeline.md): stages and what they mean, and moving a
  deal. What closing does and what winning requires, and the outcome review.
  Where a deal came from, reopening, and the 60-day rule for stuck deals. Stage
  automation, and how to read the weighted numbers.
- [Partners and commission](partners.md): making a company a partner, what each
  partner field means, and the partner list. Crediting a partner on a deal, and
  approving, paying and reversing commission.
- [Connecting your mailbox and calendar](connecting-mail-and-calendars.md):
  connecting Gmail, Outlook, an IMAP mailbox or a calendar. What to do when a
  mailbox stops syncing, and reconnecting and disconnecting. Importing your
  older mail.
- [Capture](capture.md): how email, calendar and chat get in, and what happens
  to a message step by step. The rules that file it against the right contact,
  deal and project. What happens when nothing matches, and what capture refuses
  to store.
- [Your day and your week](your-day-and-your-week.md): the bands of the
  Worklist and why a row appears there. Pinning, snoozing and what each action
  reaches. The frozen weekly review, planning a week, and what a team lead sees.
- [Performance and saved reports](sales-reporting.md): graphs, targets, scheduled editions, comparisons and exports.
- [Analytics and forecasting](analytics.md): the report sections, what each
  number covers, and how to open the deals behind it. Forecast calls and the
  projected landing. The pipeline needed figure, which is not a target.
- [Writing and sending mail](sending-mail.md): the composer, replying to one
  message in a thread, filing a send under a project, and drafting in your
  voice. The purpose picker and the consent check. What a send refuses and what
  it only warns about, and scheduling one for later. What the recipient
  controls from their own preference page.
- [Who can see an email](who-can-see-an-email.md): record visibility against a
  message's own audience. The nine reasons one is held, and which a verdict can
  clear. The three mailbox postures, the company floor, and how to share a
  thread.
- [What the AI does, and what it does not](what-the-ai-does.md): what the AI
  makes for you: drafts, document reads and the overnight brief. How every claim
  it works out carries its evidence.
- [AI providers](ai-providers.md): what each provider needs. That is a key, a
  host for an OpenAI-compatible service or decision model, or the OpenRouter
  hosts that keep data in the EU. Gemini on Vertex AI needs a location.
- [Agents, passports and what they may do](agents-and-passports.md): the two
  tiers and where the line falls. Why sending is not held behind a confirmation,
  and what protects it instead. The rule that your edits win field by field,
  what an agent is refused outright, and passports.
- [Approvals](approvals.md): what a staged action looks like, and the kinds
  that reach the inbox. How to accept, edit or reject one, and who is allowed to
  decide. The 72-hour expiry, and the one kind that never expires.
- [Offers and the rate card](offers-and-products.md): what an offer holds, and
  how its money is worked out. The four actions and what each locks, and
  recurring lines. Why a rate card change never changes an offer you already
  sent.
- [Contracts and invoices](contracts-and-invoices.md): the two value bases, and

- [Deal Rooms](deal-rooms.md): the page for one deal that the buyer sees. What

  one-time links, what a buyer never sees, and what an agent may not touch.
- [Documents and files](documents-and-files.md): documents on a company,

- [What is kept, what is destroyed](retention-exports-and-deletion.md): the six

- [Seats, roles and who can see what](seats-roles-and-access.md): full and read

- [Relationships, introductions and research](relationships-and-research.md):

- [Your own settings](your-own-settings.md): changing your password, display

  What the last 24 hours of your mail turned into.
- [Settings](settings.md): how to open Settings, every settings page and which
  one you need. Whose state each changes, and which permission opens it.

## How this handbook is written

Where the product refuses to do something, this handbook says so and says why.

Where a number is stated here, it is the number the product uses. Where a
screen in the app and this handbook disagree, the handbook says which one
describes what you will get.
